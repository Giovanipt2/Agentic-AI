# Executive Summary

Our research evaluates modern Small Language Models (SLMs) on major Brazilian university entrance exams, including ENEM, FUVEST (USP), and Unicamp. We replaced visual assets with text descriptions to isolate reasoning and used a zero-shot prompting protocol for strict JSON responses (p. 1). 

Qwen3.5-4B achieved an accuracy of 86.08% across all subjects tested, including FUVEST and Unicamp subjects from 2018 to 2024 (p. 6, 8). This model demonstrated strong performance, surpassing expectations and closing the performance gap with larger models (p. 1). Our granular analysis reveals that Qwen3.5-4B excels in specific disciplines and cognitive capabilities, with a detailed cost-benefit analysis delineating the Pareto frontier (p. 1).

Qwen3.5-4B's performance penalty for visual questions was minimal, with no significant drop in accuracy when textual descriptions were used (p. 6, 8). This study provides valuable insights into the capabilities and limitations of SLMs in evaluating university entrance exams, offering a comprehensive evaluation and empirical assessment (p. 1).
