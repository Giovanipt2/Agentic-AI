# Executive Summary

This research paper evaluates modern Small Language Models (SLMs) on major Brazilian university entrance exams, including ENEM, FUVEST (USP), and Unicamp. The study replaces visual questions with textual descriptions to isolate reasoning and uses a zero-shot prompting protocol to elicit strict JSON responses. The main contributions are:

1. **Comprehensive Evaluation**: The paper evaluates SLMs on major Brazilian university entrance exams, providing a granular analysis of model performance across specific disciplines and cognitive capabilities.
2. **Performance Penalty Analysis**: The study assesses the performance penalty incurred by replacing visual questions with textual descriptions, highlighting the impact on model accuracy.
3. **Cost-Benefit Analysis**: A detailed cost-benefit analysis delineates the Pareto frontier, offering insights into the trade-offs between model size and performance.

The evaluation results show that Qwen3.5-4B achieved an accuracy of 86.08% across all subjects and years tested (2018-2024). This comprehensive approach, including the adaptation of visual assets to text descriptions, effectively closes the performance gap with larger models, demonstrating the potential of SLMs in evaluating university entrance exams.
